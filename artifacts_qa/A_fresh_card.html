What is the molarity of a solution prepared by dissolving $4\text{ g}$ of $\text{NaOH}$ in water to form $250\text{ mL}$ of solution? (Molar mass of $\text{NaOH} = 40\text{ g/mol}$)
Step-by-Step Solution:
Moles of $\text{NaOH} = \frac{4\text{ g}}{40\text{ g/mol}} = 0.1\text{ mol}$. Volume in Liters $= \frac{250}{1000} = 0.25\text{ L}$. Molarity $M = \frac{0.1\text{ mol}}{0.25\text{ L}} = 0.4\text{ M}$.
